The process

From surplus to served
in four simple steps.

01

List surplus

Donors submit food type, quantity, freshness and pickup availability through the platform.

02

AI matches

The NGO Matching Agent evaluates nearby NGOs using food requirements, capacity and distance.

03

Confirm pickup

The Pickup Coordination Agent proposes collection arrangements for donor and NGO confirmation.

04

Track impact

The Impact Monitoring Agent records completed donations, meals rescued and waste diverted.
