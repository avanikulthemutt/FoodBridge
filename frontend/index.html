From surplus to served in four steps

Listing takes under a minute. Matching, pickup and tracking happen on their own.

  1. 1

    List surplus

  2. 2

    AI matches

    The engine ranks nearby NGOs by need, capacity, freshness and distance.

  3. 3

    Confirm pickup

    The NGO accepts, and a volunteer or driver is assigned with live tracking.

  4. 4

    Track impact

    Both sides get a receipt with meals served and waste avoided.
